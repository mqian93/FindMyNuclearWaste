# <small> Fall 2025 Cornell Data Journal Project - "Buried Truths: Mapping America’s Nuclear Waste Problem" </small>

## Summary
<p style="line-height: 1.8;">
Nuclear waste supplies ~20% of U.S. electricity, but leaves behind high-level radioactive waste that stays dangerous for over 300,000 years. With no permanent national repository, 90,000+ metric tons now sit in 70+ temporary sites across the country, often near rivers, towns, and major population centers. This raises a critical question: who lives closest to these sites, and who bears the risk? Our project investigates exactly that. Read our article here: <a href="">Temp Link</a>.
</p>

## Repository Information
<p style="line-height: 1.8;">
FindMyNuclearWaste includes the data used in all of our findings/visuals. Data retrived was from the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (site locations/images), the U.S. Department of Energy’s 2024 Spent Nuclear Fuel and Reprocessing Waste (nuclear fuel usage), and the U.S. Census Bureau's 2024 American Community Survey (Demographic/racial information). All of the code/notebooks used to display and map our data is included. Visuals included bar, bubble, pie, cloropleth, lollipop, and correlation coefficent charts. Some of these visuals are located in the repository. The final article is also provided.
</p>
